The workflow

Models can paint.
Spritewright keeps the set in order.

01

Generate with direction

Every brief inherits the project's art direction, so reeds, ducks and lanterns share one camera and one light.

02

Inspect the cut

Alpha coverage, opaque bounds, crop warnings and SHA-256 are recorded before anyone looks.

03

Animate in the lab

Order frames or slice sprite sheets, preview the loop and save a manifest without touching the sources.

04

Approve by hand

Candidates stay candidates. Only a person promotes an asset, and its prompt and lineage travel with it.
